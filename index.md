# Privacy Policy

- **Game:** Color trail behind a black ball Relax (the "Game")
- **Developer:** Michail Eshmakov, independent developer ("we", "us")
- **Publisher:** Mediacube
- **Effective date:** November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Game uses when you play it as a Facebook Instant Game, why it is used, where it is stored, and how you can have it deleted.

## What we collect

The Game does not use Facebook Login and does not ask for any Facebook permissions. It does not read your name, profile photo, email address, friends list, location or contacts.

The Game works with the following information only:

- **Game progress**: which levels you have completed and the level you reached. It is saved so that you can continue where you stopped. It is stored in the player data storage that Facebook provides to Instant Games and is linked to the game-specific player ID that Facebook assigns. We do not run our own servers and do not keep a copy.
- **Interface language**: the language setting of your Facebook app, used to choose the language of the Game. It is read on your device and is not stored.
- **Gameplay events**: when you win or lose a level, the Game sends an event with the level number to Facebook app events. We use these statistics only in aggregated form, to see which levels are too hard or too easy.
- **Advertising**: the Game shows ads through Meta Audience Network. To show and measure ads, Meta may process information about your device and your interaction with ads. This is done by Meta under its own policies. We do not receive this information.

## How we use it

- To save and restore your progress in the Game.
- To show the Game in your language.
- To improve the levels based on aggregated win and defeat statistics.
- To show ads, which keep the Game free.

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone other than Meta, as described above.

## Third parties

All data listed above is processed by Meta Platforms, Inc. as the operator of Facebook and Instant Games. Its handling is described in the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## How long it is kept

Game progress is kept until you remove the Game from your Facebook account. Gameplay events and advertising data are kept by Meta according to the Meta Privacy Policy.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your Game data at any time. The steps are described on the [data deletion page](../rainball-data-deletion/).

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. Facebook accounts are available only to people aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your rights

We do not keep any information about you ourselves, so there is nothing we can show, correct or delete on our side. Your game progress is deleted when you remove the Game from your Facebook account, as described on the [data deletion page](../rainball-data-deletion/). Information processed by Meta can be managed under the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
